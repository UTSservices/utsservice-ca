Questions

Before you reach out

How do I request service?

Use the online request form, email Admin@utsservice.ca, or call 647-777-7411.

How fast can you get here?

Building emergencies get priority. Call and we'll give you a real arrival time on the phone.

Do you quote before starting?

Always. You'll know the price before we touch a door.

Which areas do you cover?

Toronto, Peel, York, Durham and Halton regions, plus Hamilton, Niagara, Kitchener-Waterloo, Guelph, Brantford and Barrie.

What should I put in the request?

Your name and phone number, the building address, the kind of property, which doors or systems need work and how urgent it is. The more detail you give, the better we can prepare before we arrive.
