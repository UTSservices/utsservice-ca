Questions

Questions people ask us

How fast can you get here?

Building emergencies get priority. Call and we'll give you a real arrival time on the phone.

What counts as a building emergency?

An entrance that will not lock, a door that will not open, an automatic door stuck open or shut, an access system that is down, or a break-in that left a door unsecured.

What should I do while I wait?

Keep someone at the unsecured door if you can, do not force a jammed automatic door, and note which door or reader has the problem so we arrive with the right parts.
