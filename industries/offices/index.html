Offices and corporate

Access control and locks for offices

Card and fob access by floor, master key systems, and a record of who came in.

The short answer

UTS Service sets up and manages access for offices across the GTA: card and fob entry by floor or department, master key systems for the rooms that still use keys, and entry records that show who came in and when.

What we handle

The door problems this kind of building has.

01

Access by floor or department

Each person gets into the areas they need and nothing more.

02

Staff changes

A card is switched off the day someone leaves. No locks to change.

03

Master key systems

For server rooms, storage and offices that still use keys.

04

Entry records

A log of which credential opened which door, and when.

Questions

Questions people ask us

Can access be limited by floor or department?

Yes. Each card or fob is set to open only the doors that person needs, and that can be changed at any time without touching the hardware.

What happens when an employee leaves?

Their card or fob is switched off in the system. If they also held keys, the affected locks are rekeyed.
