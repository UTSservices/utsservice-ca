Public sector

Door hardware for public buildings

Code-compliant hardware and crews who know how to work in public buildings.

The short answer

UTS Service installs and maintains locks, access control and automatic doors in public buildings across the GTA. We use code-compliant hardware, keep entrances accessible, and work around the people who use the building every day.

What we handle

The door problems this kind of building has.

01

Code-compliant hardware

Locks and exit devices that meet the requirements for your building.

02

Accessible entrances

Power operators and push buttons that make an entrance barrier-free.

03

High-traffic doors

Hardware chosen for doors that open thousands of times a week.

04

Occupied buildings

Work planned so the building stays open and secure while we are on site.

Public and non-profit names we've worked for include the City of Toronto, the Ontario Provincial Police, Canada Post and Fred Victor.

Questions

Questions people ask us

Is your work code compliant?

Yes. We install hardware that meets fire code and accessibility requirements, and we'll tell you if something you have doesn't.

Can you work while the building is open?

Yes. We plan the work so entrances stay usable and secure while the building is occupied.
