Retail and franchise

Storefront locks and doors for retail and franchises

Same standard at every location. Storefront locks, panic bars and doors that open on time.

The short answer

UTS Service keeps storefront doors and locks working for retailers and franchise operators across the GTA. We repair and replace storefront locks, closers and panic bars, rekey after staff changes, and apply the same hardware standard at every location.

What we handle

The door problems this kind of building has.

01

Storefront doors and locks

Glass and aluminum storefront doors that close, latch and lock at the end of the night.

02

Panic bars and exit hardware

Exit devices that let people out and keep the door secure from outside.

03

Staff turnover

Rekeys and restricted keys so a former employee’s key stops working.

04

Every location, one vendor

One account and one standard across all your stores.

Retail and franchise names we've worked for include Tim Hortons, Domino's Pizza, Pizza Pizza, Louis Vuitton, CF Toronto Eaton Centre and SmartCentres.

Questions

Questions people ask us

Can you look after all of our locations?

Yes. One account covers every store, with the same team and the same hardware standard at each site.

A manager left with keys. What should we do?

Rekey the locks they had keys for. It is quick, costs less than new locks, and with restricted keys you control who can make copies from then on.
