Who we are

UTS Service is a commercial security company based at 50 Alness St in North York, Ontario. We provide locksmith services, access control systems, door repair and automatic door installation for businesses, commercial properties and apartment buildings.

Our mission

Protect businesses. Give people peace of mind.

Our mission is to deliver reliable, high-quality security that protects businesses, with skilled people and proven hardware matched to what each customer needs.
