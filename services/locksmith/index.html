Questions

Questions people ask us

What is a master key system?

A master key system lets one key open many locks while every other key opens only its own door. A property manager or superintendent carries the master. Tenants, staff or departments each get a key that works on their door and nothing else.

Should I rekey a lock or replace it?

Rekey when the lock works fine but you no longer trust who has keys. The pins are changed so old keys stop working, and it costs less than new hardware. Replace the lock when it is worn, damaged, or you want a higher security grade.

What makes a lock high-security?

A high-security cylinder is built to resist picking, drilling and forced entry. Most also use restricted keys, which can only be copied with the owner's authorization, so you know how many keys exist.

Can you rekey a whole building?

Yes. We rekey single units at move-out and entire buildings after a change of management, a lost master key or a break-in.
