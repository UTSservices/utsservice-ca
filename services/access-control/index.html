Access control

Access control that tells you who gets in

The short answer

Access control replaces keys with credentials you can switch on and off: fobs, cards, PIN codes or fingerprints. UTS Service designs, installs and manages these systems for apartment buildings and businesses across the GTA, and takes over systems another company installed.

Questions

Questions people ask us

What happens when a fob is lost?

The lost fob is cancelled in the system and stops working right away. Nobody needs to rekey a lock or hand out new keys. A replacement fob is issued to the person who lost it.

Fob, keypad or biometric: which is right for my building?

Fobs and cards suit most apartment buildings and offices because each one belongs to a person and can be cancelled alone. Keypads suit low-risk shared doors. Biometric readers suit rooms where you need to be sure of the person, not just the credential.

Does access control keep a record of who came in?

Yes. Most systems log each entry with the credential used, the door and the time. That record helps after an incident and shows which fobs are still in use.

Can you take over our existing fob system?

Usually, yes. We'll look at what you have, tell you if it can be managed or should be replaced, and give you the price before any work starts.

What happens to the doors if the power goes out?

It depends on how each door is set up. Some locks release when power is lost so people can always get out, and others stay locked from the outside. Battery backup keeps readers working through short outages. We set this up door by door.
