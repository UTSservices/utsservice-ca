The short answer

Most door problems come from four parts: the closer, the hinges, the strike and the frame. UTS Service repairs and replaces all of them on commercial, storefront and apartment building doors across the GTA, so the door closes, latches and locks every time.

When to call

Signs it is time to call us.

  • The door slams or won't close without help
  • It doesn't latch unless you pull it
  • It drags on the floor or rubs the frame
  • The lock no longer lines up
  • You can see daylight around a closed door

Questions

Questions people ask us

Why won't my door latch?

A door that will not latch usually has a closer that needs adjusting, hinges that have sagged, or a strike plate that no longer lines up with the latch. All three can be fixed without replacing the door.

Should a commercial door be repaired or replaced?

Repair it when the door itself is sound and the problem is hardware: closer, hinges, strike or lock. Replace it when the door or frame is bent, rusted through or cracked, because new hardware on a damaged door will fail again.

What does a door closer do?

A door closer is the arm at the top of the door that pulls it shut at a controlled speed. Set properly, it closes and latches the door without slamming. A leaking or worn closer is the most common reason a door slams or stays open.

Why does it matter if a fire door latches?

A fire-rated door only does its job when it closes and latches by itself. If it is propped open, or the closer or latch has failed, it should be repaired promptly.
