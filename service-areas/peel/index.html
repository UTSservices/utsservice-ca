The buildings

What we see in Peel Region.

Peel mixes dense condo towers in Mississauga with warehouses and industrial units around Pearson and plazas across Brampton and Caledon. Heavy doors, shipping areas and shared entrances take a beating, and multi-unit buildings need tight control over who has keys and fobs.
