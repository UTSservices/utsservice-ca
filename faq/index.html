Questions

Locksmith

What is a master key system?

A master key system lets one key open many locks while every other key opens only its own door. A property manager or superintendent carries the master. Tenants, staff or departments each get a key that works on their door and nothing else.

Should I rekey a lock or replace it?

Rekey when the lock works fine but you no longer trust who has keys. The pins are changed so old keys stop working, and it costs less than new hardware. Replace the lock when it is worn, damaged, or you want a higher security grade.

What makes a lock high-security?

A high-security cylinder is built to resist picking, drilling and forced entry. Most also use restricted keys, which can only be copied with the owner's authorization, so you know how many keys exist.

Can you rekey a whole building?

Yes. We rekey single units at move-out and entire buildings after a change of management, a lost master key or a break-in.

Questions

Access control

What happens when a fob is lost?

The lost fob is cancelled in the system and stops working right away. Nobody needs to rekey a lock or hand out new keys. A replacement fob is issued to the person who lost it.

Fob, keypad or biometric: which is right for my building?

Fobs and cards suit most apartment buildings and offices because each one belongs to a person and can be cancelled alone. Keypads suit low-risk shared doors. Biometric readers suit rooms where you need to be sure of the person, not just the credential.

Does access control keep a record of who came in?

Yes. Most systems log each entry with the credential used, the door and the time. That record helps after an incident and shows which fobs are still in use.

Can you take over our existing fob system?

Usually, yes. We'll look at what you have, tell you if it can be managed or should be replaced, and give you the price before any work starts.

What happens to the doors if the power goes out?

It depends on how each door is set up. Some locks release when power is lost so people can always get out, and others stay locked from the outside. Battery backup keeps readers working through short outages. We set this up door by door.

Questions

Door repair

Why won't my door latch?

A door that will not latch usually has a closer that needs adjusting, hinges that have sagged, or a strike plate that no longer lines up with the latch. All three can be fixed without replacing the door.

Should a commercial door be repaired or replaced?

Repair it when the door itself is sound and the problem is hardware: closer, hinges, strike or lock. Replace it when the door or frame is bent, rusted through or cracked, because new hardware on a damaged door will fail again.

What does a door closer do?

A door closer is the arm at the top of the door that pulls it shut at a controlled speed. Set properly, it closes and latches the door without slamming. A leaking or worn closer is the most common reason a door slams or stays open.

Why does it matter if a fire door latches?

A fire-rated door only does its job when it closes and latches by itself. If it is propped open, or the closer or latch has failed, it should be repaired promptly.

Questions

Automatic doors

Can an existing door be made automatic?

Often, yes. A swing door operator can be fitted to many existing doors, with a push button or sensor to trigger it. We check the door, frame and power supply first to confirm it will work.

Sliding or swing: which automatic door do I need?

Sliding doors suit wide entrances with constant traffic, like lobbies and storefronts. Swing operators suit a single existing door, and cost less because the door and frame stay.

Do automatic doors need regular service?

Yes. Sensors, motors and safety features wear with use. Regular checks keep the door safe and catch problems before the entrance goes out of service.

Do push-button openers help with accessibility?

Yes. A power operator with a push button is a common way to make an entrance barrier-free, which many Ontario buildings are required to provide. We install hardware that meets the code requirements for your building.
