The Building Program

One call. Every door.

Most buildings juggle a locksmith, an access company and a door guy. Then nobody owns the problem. We take all of it.

The short answer

The Building Program is one account for every lock, reader and door in your buildings. Instead of calling a locksmith, an access control company and a door company, you call one team that already knows your properties.

What is included

Four things you stop chasing.

01

Fob management

02

Know who holds which key, and rekey when someone leaves.

03

Scheduled checks

04

Priority line

Building emergencies go to the front of the queue.

Questions

Questions people ask us

Do you work with property management companies?

Yes. Most of our work is for managers with more than one building. One account, one team, every site.

Can you take over our existing fob system?

Usually, yes. We'll look at what you have, tell you if it can be managed or should be replaced, and give you the price before any work starts.

Can one account cover several buildings?

Yes. One account covers every site, with the same team and the same standards at each.

What does a scheduled check cover?

The closers, strikes, hinges, locks and readers on your main doors, looked at on a regular visit so problems are caught before a door fails.
